Moderation - Drink Tracker

Drink a little less, without the lecture.

Moderation is a gentle drink tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. Set your own weekly goal, log a drink in one tap, and let two simple rings keep the score — synced privately through Apple Health and iCloud.

Built for real life, not resolutions

You set the numbers. Moderation just keeps them honest.

Two rings, one habit

The inner ring is today against your daily limit; the outer is the week against your goal. Goals are weekly — tighten or loosen them anytime, and past weeks keep the goals they were set under.

Log anywhere in one tap

The app, your wrist, a widget, the Action Button, Control Center, or Siri. Type and cost are optional details — add them when you care, skip them when you don't.

Dry days count too

Confirm alcohol-free days, build streaks, and set a dry-days-per-week goal. A daily summary coaches with plain numbers — never judgment.

Your data stays yours

Drinks live in Apple Health and sync through your private iCloud. No account, no servers of ours, no ads. Anonymous usage stats show which features get used — never what you drink, how much, or when.

At a glance everywhere

Home-screen and lock-screen widgets in every size, Apple Watch complications that stay current, and a full ring calendar of your history.

Spending, if you want it

Optionally track what drinks cost and see it by day, week, and month. Sometimes the wallet is the best coach.

The rings, the calendar, and the wrist — one habit, every screen.
